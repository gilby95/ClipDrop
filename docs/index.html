Install it

Run ClipDrop-Setup.exe. It adds a desktop icon and finds where your capture app saves clips on its own.

Trim it

New clips pop up at the top. Pick one and drag the green handles around the good part.

Drop it

Hit Compress for Discord, then Share to Discord, or just drag the clip into any chat.

Questions

Is it safe?

Yes. It's open source, so anyone can read the code on GitHub. The Windows warning only means the app isn't paid-signed, not that anything is wrong with it.

Does it upload my clips anywhere?

No. Your clips stay on your PC. The only time anything leaves is when you press Share to Discord, and then it goes straight to the server's channel.

Does it mess with my original recordings?

Never. It makes a new, smaller copy (in Videos\ClipDrop) and leaves the original alone.

I have Nitro. Can I post bigger clips?

Yes. Pick Nitro Basic, 50 MB or Nitro, 500 MB under "Fit under" and it keeps more quality. Posts made with Share to Discord follow the server's limit instead.

How do I uninstall it?

Windows Settings → Apps → ClipDrop → Uninstall, like any other program.
